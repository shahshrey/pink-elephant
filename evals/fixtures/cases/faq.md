# Help Center FAQ

**Can I share my account with a teammate?**
No. Each seat is for one person. Don't share your password with anyone, including Acme staff; we will never ask for it. Invite teammates from Settings → Members instead.

**Why was my account locked?**
After five failed sign-in attempts we lock the account for 60 minutes. Don't keep retrying during that window, because each attempt restarts the timer.

**Do you store card numbers?**
We don't store full card numbers. Our payment processor keeps them, and we only see the last four digits.

**Can I use a VPN?**
Yes, but avoid switching regions mid-session; it triggers a security check and signs you out.
